About New Guide

Explore. Understand. Build.

New Guide is an open research and technical publication platform exploring ideas across science, engineering, artificial intelligence, computing, physics, and emerging technologies.

The goal is simple: make complex ideas understandable while going deeper than surface-level explanations, bridging rigorous theory with practical implementation for researchers, developers, and technology enthusiasts.

What We Explore

  • Science & Physics — Theoretical concepts, mathematical models, relativity, and fundamental physics.
  • Engineering & Technology — Systems, hardware, aerospace, energy, materials, and emerging technologies.
  • Artificial Intelligence & Computing — AI agents, neuromorphic systems, machine learning architectures, and computational research.
  • Technical Guides & Tutorials — Practical, step-by-step technical guides that connect code with systems engineering.

Our Approach

New Guide combines research papers, technical references, mathematical reasoning, simulations, experiments, and engineering principles wherever appropriate.

We distinguish between established knowledge, theoretical work, computational results, experimental results, and speculative ideas. A hypothesis is not presented as a fact, and a simulation is not treated as experimental proof.

Where possible, articles provide references, methodology, code, data, or other material that allows readers to investigate the work further.

Creator & Founder

New Guide was created and founded by Vijayaramanan, a technology and research enthusiast working across artificial intelligence, data science, computing, and engineering.

While initially built to document individual research explorations, Vijayaramanan designed New Guide to serve as an open, accessible knowledge commons for everyone who loves science and deep technical engineering.

Public Publishing & Community Contributions

New Guide is an open publication platform. We actively welcome contributions, tutorials, and research breakdowns from developers, engineers, researchers, and domain specialists around the world.

Authors who publish with New Guide retain full ownership and credit for their work, complete with author bylines, biographies, and links to their personal websites or repositories. If you would like to publish an article or tutorial, visit our Publish With Us section or check out our Authoring Guidelines.

The Philosophy

Explore boldly. Reason carefully. Verify whenever possible.

New Guide is an independent platform for learning, investigation, experimentation, and open technical exploration.
