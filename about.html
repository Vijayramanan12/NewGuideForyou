About New Guide

Explore. Understand. Build.

New Guide is an independent research and technical publication exploring ideas across science, engineering, artificial intelligence, computing, physics, and emerging technologies.

The goal is simple: make complex ideas understandable while going deeper than surface-level explanations.

What We Explore

  • Science & Physics — Theoretical concepts, mathematical models, and fundamental questions.
  • Engineering & Technology — Systems, hardware, aerospace, energy, and emerging technologies.
  • Artificial Intelligence & Computing — AI, machine learning, algorithms, architectures, and computational research.
  • Technical Guides — Practical explanations that connect theory with implementation.

Our Approach

New Guide combines research papers, technical references, mathematical reasoning, simulations, experiments, and engineering principles wherever appropriate.

We distinguish between established knowledge, theoretical work, computational results, experimental results, and speculative ideas. A hypothesis is not presented as a fact, and a simulation is not treated as experimental proof.

Where possible, articles provide references, methodology, code, data, or other material that allows readers to investigate the work further.

About the Author

I’m Vijayaramanan, a technology and research enthusiast working across artificial intelligence, data science, computing, and engineering.

New Guide is a personal platform for documenting technical research, experiments, ideas, and discoveries.

The Philosophy

Explore boldly. Reason carefully. Verify whenever possible.

New Guide is not a peer-reviewed scientific journal or institutional research laboratory. It is an independent platform for learning, investigation, experimentation, and open technical exploration.
