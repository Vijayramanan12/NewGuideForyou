About New Guide

New Guide is an independent science and technology publication dedicated to deep dives, technical analysis, and research explorations across modern scientific discoveries and breakthrough engineering innovations.

Author & Lead Researcher

Vijayaramanan is the creator, writer, and lead researcher behind New Guide. Passionate about unraveling complex scientific principles, software architectures, advanced physics, and frontier technologies, Vijayaramanan writes comprehensive, accessible, and well-researched guides designed for engineers, researchers, and curious minds.

What We Cover

  • Emerging Technologies: Artificial intelligence, quantum computing, autonomous systems, next-generation web architectures, and high-performance computing.
  • Applied Engineering & Physics: Advanced propulsion systems, material sciences, renewable energy breakthroughs, and aerospace technologies.
  • Scientific Research & Analysis: Exploring peer-reviewed literature, translating theoretical equations into practical insights, and examining experimental data.
  • Technical Guides & Tutorials: Practical deep dives with reproducible code snippets, architectural breakdowns, and hands-on demonstrations.

Publishing Philosophy

At New Guide, we believe technical writing should be:

  • Clear & Accessible: Breaking down intricate scientific concepts without sacrificing technical rigor.
  • Evidence-Based: Backed by citations, experimental data, and verifiable equations.
  • Open & Reproducible: Providing open access to formulas, source code, and references.
